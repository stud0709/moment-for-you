# A moment for you v1.0.4

- Stops reminders while Windows is locked, a screensaver is running, or the PC is asleep. Returning starts a **full new reminder interval**.
- Waits until all active away conditions end before restarting. Manual pauses remain in effect until you choose Resume reminders.
- Clicking **Project homepage** acts as **Done**, dismissing all break windows before opening the browser.
- Places **v1.0.4 · © stud0709 · Project homepage** in the lower-right corner of the green screen, matching the “Take your time” font and size.
- Checks the footer at common monitor sizes and scaling settings, including 1920×1200 at 125%.
- Compresses the bundled runtime to reduce the portable executable size.

The packaged app passes 20 automated checks, including restart behavior, overlapping away conditions, homepage navigation, and footer placement.

Download the **win-x64.zip**, extract it, and run **A moment for you.exe**, or download **A.moment.for.you.exe** directly. The portable Windows x64 build includes its runtime. Exit the previous copy from its tray menu before launching this version; your saved interval is preserved.
